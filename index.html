About Us

Quietly keeping NC homes comfortable for 15+ years.

Titanium Barrier Pest Control was founded with a simple idea: pest control shouldn’t feel chaotic. It should feel calm, predictable, and protective—like a steady barrier around your home.

Over the last 15 years, we’ve helped thousands of families in Raleigh, Durham, Cary, and surrounding towns handle everything from routine ants to complex termite and rodent situations. Many of our customers have stayed with us through new homes, new pets, and new chapters in life.

Our mission

We exist to protect homes and the people inside them with solutions that are:

  • Safe and thoughtful
  • Reliable and consistent
  • Focused on long-term prevention
  • Transparent and easy to understand

The Titanium Barrier concept

The Titanium Barrier is our premium protective approach: a layered system of exterior treatments, entry-point sealing, and ongoing monitoring that keeps pests from getting comfortable in your living spaces.
